About Washington Wage Tools

Free calculators for Washington workers to check their pay, overtime, and minimum wage compliance.

Who we are

We built this site because Washington State has one of the most progressive minimum wage laws in the country — and the numbers change every year. Whether you're in Seattle, Spokane, or a small town east of the mountains, knowing what you should be earning isn't always straightforward.

What we offer

  • Washington Hourly Wage Calculator — convert any hourly rate to annual salary, weekly, and monthly pay.
  • Washington Overtime Calculator — calculate time-and-a-half pay for hours over 40 in a week.
  • Seattle Hourly Wage Calculator — Seattle has its own higher minimum wage for large employers.
  • Seattle Minimum Wage Guide — full breakdown of Seattle's 2026 wage rates by employer size.
  • Washington Sales Tax Calculator — estimate sales tax on purchases anywhere in the state.

Data sources

Washington State minimum wage figures are sourced from the Washington State Department of Labor & Industries (L&I). Seattle's local minimum wage data comes from the Seattle Office of Labor Standards. We update our figures annually when new rates take effect each January.

Important disclaimers

Our calculators provide estimates for general guidance only. They are not legal or financial advice. Actual pay may depend on your employer, job type, tips, overtime rules, and local ordinances. For wage disputes or official inquiries, contact Washington State L&I directly.
