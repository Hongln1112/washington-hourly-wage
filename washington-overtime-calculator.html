How overtime works in Washington

Washington state follows the federal Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) rule: most non-exempt employees earn 1.5× their regular rate (time-and-a-half) for hours worked beyond 40 in a single workweek.

  • First 40 hours are paid at your regular hourly rate.
  • Hours over 40 are paid at 1.5× your regular rate.
  • Some salaried employees and certain industries are exempt — check with your employer to confirm your status.

Example

If you earn $17.13/hour and work 45 hours in a week: your first 40 hours = $685.20, and the 5 overtime hours at 1.5× ($25.70) = $128.51. Your weekly pay ≈ $813.71.

Is overtime pay taxable?

Yes. Overtime earnings are treated as ordinary wages — federal, state, and FICA taxes still apply. The calculator above shows pre-tax overtime pay.
