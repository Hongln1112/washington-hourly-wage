Seattle Minimum Wage Guide

Seattle has one of the highest minimum wages in the country. Here's what you need to know as a worker or employer in 2026.

Seattle Minimum Wage 2026

Seattle's minimum wage is higher than both the Washington State rate and the federal rate. It also depends on the size of your employer:

Employer size2026 minimum wage
Large employers (500+ employees)$20.76/hour
Small employers (500 or fewer)$18.76/hour

Rates effective January 1, 2026. Tips and service charges do not count toward the minimum wage.

How is Seattle different from the rest of Washington?

The statewide minimum wage is $17.13/hour for 2026. Seattle's rate is $2–$3 higher because the city passed its own ordinance in 2014, phasing in a $15/hour minimum wage over several years. Today, Seattle has one of the highest mandated wages in the United States.

What if I'm paid below minimum wage?

If you believe your employer is paying you below the Seattle minimum wage, you can file a complaint with the Seattle Office of Labor Standards. They investigate wage theft and can order back pay, penalties, and legal fees.
