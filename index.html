How to calculate your hourly rate in Washington

If you know your annual salary, your hourly rate is simple to work out:

Hourly rate = Annual salary ÷ (Hours per week × Weeks per year)

For example, a salary of $52,000 at 40 hours per week and 52 weeks per year gives an hourly rate of about $25.00.

Use the calculator above to run your own numbers — it works both directions, so you can also see what an hourly rate adds up to across a full year.

Washington's minimum wage in 2026

Washington State sets its minimum wage by law, and it is adjusted each January for inflation. These are the rates in effect for 2026:

If your calculated hourly rate comes in below the minimum for your situation, it's a sign your pay may not meet Washington law — though some cities like Seattle set a higher local minimum.
