Washington's minimum wage in 2026

Washington State sets its minimum wage by law, adjusted each January for inflation. These are the rates in effect for 2026:

2026 Minimum wage (16+)
$17.13 / hr
Under 16 (85%)
$14.56 / hr
Effective date
Jan 1, 2026

If your calculated hourly rate comes in below the minimum, it may mean your pay doesn't meet Washington law — though some cities like Seattle set a higher local minimum.
