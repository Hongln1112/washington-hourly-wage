Washington sales tax rates by city

Washington State's base sales tax is 6.5%. Local cities and counties add their own taxes, so the total you pay depends on where you shop:

Seattle: ~10.25%
Spokane: ~9.1%
Tacoma: ~10.3%
Vancouver: ~8.4%
Bellevue: ~10.1%

For the exact rate in your city, check the Washington State Department of Revenue website.
