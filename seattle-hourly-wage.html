Seattle's 2026 minimum wage

Seattle sets its own minimum wage that is higher than both Washington state and the federal floor. In 2026, the Seattle minimum wage is $20.76 per hour for most businesses. This rate adjusts annually based on inflation (CPI-W).

  • $20.76/hour — 2026 Seattle citywide minimum wage
  • $17.13/hour — 2026 Washington state minimum wage (lower than Seattle's)
  • $7.25/hour — federal minimum wage (much lower; Seattle overrides it)

How to calculate your hourly rate

Divide your annual salary by your total hours per year. For a standard 40-hour, 52-week schedule, that's 2,080 hours. For example, a $45,000 salary ÷ 2,080 = ~$21.63/hour, which meets Seattle's minimum wage.

Important notes

Seattle's minimum wage can differ slightly by business size and whether the employer contributes toward medical benefits. Always confirm the exact rate with your employer or the Seattle Office of Labor Standards.
