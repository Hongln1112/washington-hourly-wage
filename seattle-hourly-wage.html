Seattle's 2026 minimum wage

Seattle sets its own minimum wage that is higher than both Washington state and the federal floor. In 2026, the Seattle minimum wage is $20.76 per hour for most businesses. This rate adjusts annually based on inflation (CPI-W).

  • $20.76/hour — 2026 Seattle citywide minimum wage (large employers)
  • $17.13/hour — 2026 Washington state minimum wage (lower than Seattle's)
  • $7.25/hour — federal minimum wage (much lower; Seattle overrides it)

How to calculate your hourly rate

Divide your annual salary by your total hours per year. For a standard 40-hour, 52-week schedule, that's 2,080 hours. For example, a $45,000 salary ÷ 2,080 = ~$21.63/hour, which meets Seattle's minimum wage. After taxes, that hourly rate translates to roughly $17–$18/hour in actual take-home pay.

Important notes

Seattle's minimum wage can differ slightly by business size and whether the employer contributes toward medical benefits. Small employers (500 or fewer employees) have a lower rate of $18.76/hour. Always confirm the exact rate with your employer or the Seattle Office of Labor Standards.
